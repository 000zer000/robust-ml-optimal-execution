## Inclusion hierarchy

1. Official journal or conference publication.
2. Official publisher-hosted proceedings or author manuscript corresponding to a published work.
3. Official arXiv/SSRN preprint when no peer-reviewed version was established or when the work is very recent.
4. Surveys only for orientation and vocabulary.

Blogs, marketing pages, unsourced repositories, and secondary summaries were not used as foundations for technical claims.

## Source-integrity rules for later writing

- A paper may support a design motivation without proving the project's future result.
- Preprint results must be labelled as preprint evidence.
- No citation may be added from title memory alone; metadata must be verified before the paper is released.
- Related-work prose must distinguish prediction, execution, simulation, and causal impact claims.
- The final paper bibliography must be rechecked against publisher metadata before release.
