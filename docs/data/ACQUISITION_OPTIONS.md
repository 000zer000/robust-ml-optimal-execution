# Step 11 acquisition options

No purchase has been made. The choice of paid access remains an explicit user decision.

## Option A — self-capture official Binance data

**Cost:** exchange access is public; infrastructure/storage cost remains.  
**Time:** at least 100 validated whole days, with a planned 120 calendar-day run.  
**Evidence quality:** strongest provenance and raw sequence evidence.  
**Risk:** delays the full empirical study and requires reliable 24/7 infrastructure.

This is the canonical fallback and can begin as soon as Step 12 is implemented.

## Option B — Tardis one-off purchase

Request a quote for only Binance Spot, BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT, incremental L2 and trades, and a range large
enough to yield 100 valid days. The site advertises one-off purchases with a USD 300 minimum, but
the exact quote and whether raw exchange-native replay is included must be confirmed in writing.

This is the preferred paid option if the quote is materially below a subscription and includes the
required representation and licence.

## Option C — Tardis Academic Spot subscription

The 2026-08-06 public pricing page lists USD 450/month, quarterly or yearly billing only. The billing
FAQ says a quarterly subscription includes 12 months of historical access and Academic/Solo tiers
provide downloadable CSV only. The inferred quarterly amount is USD 1,350 before tax, but the
checkout quote is authoritative.

This accelerates historical work but normalized CSV does not expose every exchange-native sequence
field and does not remove the need for a live raw pilot.

## Option D — free monthly sample days

The first day of each month is available without an API key. Use these files for parser, schema, and
book-reconstruction development. They are not sufficient for the 100-day empirical protocol and
must not be presented as the final dataset.

## Approval rule

Only Othmane can approve a paid purchase. Approval must state:

- maximum total price including tax;
- one-off or subscription;
- date range;
- exact symbols and data types;
- CSV or raw replay;
- cancellation/renewal terms;
- licence/publication constraints accepted.
