# Step 28 claim boundaries

Step 28 may claim that the robustness machinery exists, is deterministic on the engineering fixture,
and exposes strategy-rank changes under controlled synthetic perturbations.

It may not claim:

- historical robustness or real-market generalisation;
- a calibrated central queue model;
- that decision-grid proxies are literal 10/50/100/250/1000 ms measurements;
- that horizon proxies are literal 30/60/300 s research episodes;
- formal statistical significance, confidence intervals, or multiplicity-adjusted findings;
- hardware-normalized inference performance, CPU/GPU superiority, or a CUDA conclusion;
- that PPO, ML-MPC, or any other method is the final winning strategy.

Historically observed, synthetic engineering, and adversarial synthetic evidence remain separately
labelled. The locked historical test remains unopened.
